\begin{answer}
The ASWZ lattice data for the Collins--Soper (CS) kernel now enter the \alphas\ fit as an exact $\chi^2$ term, with the
kernel computed by SCETlib at every fit step. In the proposed nominal, \fit{LATFROZ\_V3}, the lattice constrains only
the CS non-perturbative (NP) parameters \lnu2, \lnu4. Against the Z-only fits it moves \alphas\ by $+0.245\,\sigma$ when
\lnu4\ floats ($\sigma(\alphas)\times1.05$) and by $-0.013\,\sigma$ when $\lnu4=0$. The Z data fix the product of the CS
and TMD damping, and the lattice decides how that product is split between the two.
\textbf{Caveat:} the Z data and the lattice disagree on the shape of $\gz(b_T)$ at $3.2\sigma$ (PG $=13.5$ for
2\,dof). Quote it with any lattice-constrained \alphas. Real data; blinded shifts in units of
the reference fit's $\sigma(\alphas)$.
\end{answer}

\section{Motivation and question}

At small \qT\ the Z spectrum depends on the CS kernel $\gz(b_T)$ and on the TMD boundary condition $F^\mathrm{NP}(b_T,Y)$.
At $Q=m_Z$ the data see essentially one combination of the two. A gen-level Fisher forecast at the physical point gives
$\rho(\lnu2,\Lambda_2)\approx-0.985$ and $\sigma(\lnu2)=0.25\,\mathrm{GeV}^2$ with the NP parameters free, and splitting
$m_{\ell\ell}$ into windows does not break the degeneracy \taskref{260923-qsplit-fisher}. Avkhadiev, Shanahan, Wagman and
Zhao (ASWZ, \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.06725}{arXiv:2402.06725}) compute $\gz$ on the lattice at
$0.1\lesssim b_T\lesssim0.9$\,fm. Can they constrain SCETlib's CS kernel in the \alphas\ fit, and what
do they do to \alphas? A 1D Gaussian prior on \lnu2\ became an exact $\chi^2$ term, compared with lattice-free fits.

\section{Setup and method}

\paragraph{Fits.} All fits use real data and card~A, the 2D $\ptll\times\yll$ Z$\to\mu\mu$ fit
(\code{260916_Z_2D_card_adcorr}); the prediction is the SCETlib-AD param model (CT18ZNNLO, N$^3$LL+NNLO, cache
\code{pdf62_y35_260921}, its $|Y|\le2.5$ subset, which is bitwise identical for card~A). The fits float \alphas, the TMD
$\Lambda_2,\Lambda_4,\Delta\Lambda_2$, the CS \lnu2, \lnu4, nine resummation TNPs, \code{resumTransition2} and 29 PDF
eigenvectors (44 model priors; $\lambda_\infty^\nu=2$ frozen). The NP damping wall (relu$^2$, margin 0) runs at $\tau=8$
(``STIFF''). A lattice term replaces the registry priors on
\lnu2, \lnu4. \fit{LATFROZ} is warm-started from \fit{LATB8}. \alphas\ is blinded throughout.
\textbf{Fit names.} \fit{XWSTIFF}, \fit{XL4ZSTIFF}: Z only, \lnu4\ free or $=0$. \fit{NOMSTIFF}: old nominal, a 1D
Gaussian on \lnu2\ from a lattice-only fit, $\lnu4=0$. \fit{LATB8}: exact term, \emph{live} kernel.
\fit{LATFROZ\_V\{1,2,3\}}: exact term, \emph{frozen} kernel, $n_f$ row V1/V2/V3 (Table~\ref{tab:nf}).

\paragraph{The lattice data.} ASWZ give 21 points $y_i=\gamma_q(b_i,\mu=2\,\mathrm{GeV})$ in $\overline{\mathrm{MS}}$,
$n_f=4$, on three ensembles ($a=0.15$, $0.12$, $0.09$\,fm) with a covariance each. $\gamma_q$ is exactly SCETlib's
$\gz=\tfrac12\tilde\gamma_\nu$; the only scheme mismatch is $n_f=4$ against our $n_f=5$ \taskref{260923-conventions-map}.
We use the per-ensemble points with the authors' artefact term $k_1a/b_T$, which reproduces their fit
\taskref{260923-lattice-data-refit}. Cross-ensemble correlations are negligible (authors), so $C$ is
block-diagonal \logref{2026-09-29}. Our NP form
$\gz^\mathrm{NP}=-\tfrac{\lambda_\infty^\nu}{2}\tanh[(\lnu2 b_T^2+\lnu4 b_T^4)/\lambda_\infty^\nu]$ describes the lattice
alone with $\chi^2=6.7/18$ \taskref{260923-scetlib-kernel-fit}.

\paragraph{The lattice term and how it works.} The term is a rabbit regularizer (\code{-r}) on the Z fit's own parameters
$p$. It introduces no new parameter. It adds to the NLL
\begin{equation}
  \tfrac12\big[\chi^2_\mathrm{lat}(p)-\chi^2_\mathrm{min}\big],\qquad
  \chi^2_\mathrm{lat}=\min_{k_1} r^\mathsf{T}C^{-1}r,\qquad
  r_i=\gz^\mathrm{SCETlib}(b_i,2\,\mathrm{GeV};p)+k_1\,a_i/b_i-y_i ,
\end{equation}
with $k_1$ profiled analytically. Everything that depends on a convention is fixed when
rabbit arms the term (Fig.~\ref{fig:pipeline}). At each step only the kernel (21 points with exact clad gradient and
Hessian, 3.4\,ms) and a $21\times21$ quadratic form are recomputed. The kernel is the same C++ function that the cross
section's AD kernel calls, and a load-time check verifies that its coefficients are bitwise those of the loaded cache
\taskref{261007-lattice-term-native}. In \emph{frozen} mode (\fit{LATFROZ}) the perturbative part is evaluated at
$\alphas(m_Z)=0.1168$ and CS TNPs $=0$. That coupling corresponds to the lattice's own
$\alpha_s^{(4)}(2\,\mathrm{GeV})=0.293$. Only \lnu2\ and \lnu4\ reach the kernel, and the term's derivative with respect
to \alphas\ and every TNP is exactly zero, checked inside the fitter \taskref{261008-latfroz-nf-variants}. The live mode
(\fit{LATB8}) would need the points' own \alphas\ dependence, which is not published.

\paragraph{The $n_f$ systematic row.} The alternative kernel puts $n_f=4$ into $\beta$, the cusp and/or the boundary
term, with $\alpha_s^{(4)}:=\alpha_s^{(5)}$ at an identification scale. Its point shift (NP part cancels exactly) is mapped
onto $(\lnu2,\lnu4)$ by a lattice-only refit and enters $C$ as one row (Table~\ref{tab:nf}). V3 is the lattice's own
perturbative theory: $n_f=4$ throughout, coupling from $m_b$. (A literal \code{nfmatch=4.18} would run up in $n_f=5$
and down in $n_f=4$, flipping the sign of the shift; not fitted.) \taskref{261008-latfroz-nf-variants}

\subsection{The Z data fix the product of CS and TMD damping; the lattice moves it to the CS side}




At $m_Z$, $Y=0$ the total NP damping (CS factor $\times F^\mathrm{NP}$) of the Z-only and joint fits agree to
$\le2.6\,\%$ wherever the product exceeds 0.2 ($b_T\lesssim1.5\,\mathrm{GeV}^{-1}$). Yet the two factors separately differ
by a factor 5--10 at $b_T=2\,\mathrm{GeV}^{-1}$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:npf}) \taskref{261008-np-function-figures}. This is the
$\rho(\lnu2,\Lambda_2)\approx-0.985$ degeneracy made visible: the lattice moves damping from the TMD boundary condition
to the CS kernel at no cost to the Z shape. The joint $F^\mathrm{NP}(Y=0)$ falls to 0.1 already at
$b_T\approx2.0\,\mathrm{GeV}^{-1}$, against $b_T\approx3.1\,\mathrm{GeV}^{-1}$ for Z only and $3.4\,\mathrm{GeV}^{-1}$ for MAP22. The lattice changes the NP
\emph{decomposition} while the measured \qT\ shape, and with it \alphas, stays put: hence the neutrality at fixed \lnu4.
The decomposition matters wherever the Z tune is carried to other $Q$, $Y$, or to the W.

\section{Robustness, systematics and caveats}

\alphas\ barely depends on how the lattice is applied. All exact-term variants lie within $-0.15\ldots-0.04\,\snom$ of
\fit{NOMSTIFF} \taskref{261006-lattice-chi2-in-fit}\,\taskref{261007-lattice-term-native}. Freezing the kernel moves
\alphas\ by $-0.026\,\snom$ relative to the live \fit{LATB8} ($\sigma$ $+0.9\,\%$). The $n_f$ conventions span
$0.009\,\snom$ (V2 vs V3: $0.0014\,\snom$), and Newton steps predicted every \fit{LATFROZ} fit to $\le0.0005\,\snom$
\taskref{261008-latfroz-nf-variants}. The dropped $b_T$ window moved \alphas\ by $\le0.0004\,\sigma$; replacing the old exact-RGE table by SCETlib's
analytic-RGE kernel, by $-0.027\,\snom$ \taskref{261007-lattice-term-native}.
 The fits used the relu$^2$ wall; the WRemnants
default is now a $C^2$ ramp (\code{692f9483}), so reruns need \code{smooth=relu2}. \fit{LATB8}'s full saturated test is
753.35/777 ($p=72\,\%$); the projected-\ptll\ test is poor, as in every fit of this lineage: 78.4/39 ($p=0.019\,\%$), and
80.7/39 without the exact term \taskref{261007-lattice-term-native}. Not covered: $\lambda_\infty^\nu$ (held at 2), the
$n_f$ $4\to3$ step below $m_c$, and the authors' matching order (assumed inside their errors).

\section{Conclusions and open items}

The lattice enters the \alphas\ fit exactly and cheaply, constrains only the CS NP sector, and is close to neutral for
\alphas\ once \lnu4\ is treated alike. The price is a $3.2\sigma$ shape disagreement with the Z data, which goes with the
result.
