\begin{answer}
Two slow-downs of the walled SCETlib-AD fits were diagnosed and fixed; neither fix moves the minimum. (1)~The
hours-long ``crawl'' of the main fit is a limit cycle of scipy trust-krylov's radius rule at the curvature jump of the
\relu\ NP damping wall; a curvature-continuous (\Ctwo) ramp removes it (from the same stuck point: reference minimum in 46
iterations, 1.1\,h; \relu\ still 430\,NLL above it after 219), with $\dalphas=+3.9\times10^{-6}\,\sigma$. (2)~The projected-\ptll\ saturated sub-fit is slow because \alphas\ is 97.9\,\% degenerate
with the 39 free bin scales; full-scope spectral preconditioning reaches the same minimum ($\Delta$loss
$-1.7\times10^{-10}$) with 614 instead of 2150 Hessian-vector products, about $3\times$ faster at equal node load. Both
are now defaults. \textbf{Caveat:} one real start each; preconditioning is unmeasured on toys and on the main fit.
Real data, \alphas\ blinded; shifts in units of the reference fit's $\sigma(\alphas)$.
\end{answer}

\section{Motivation and question}

The walled NP fits (a rabbit regularizer keeps the SCETlib NP parameters in the physically damped region) minimise with
scipy's trust-krylov, and some sat for hours on a slowly falling plateau that looked like a converged fit: the census
fit \fit{CENS03} had not converged after 390 iterations \taskref{261006-diagnosis}, and \fit{CMR1A} spent 1.5\,h on a
plateau before escaping \logref{2026-10-06}. trust-constr holds the constraints exactly but did not converge from distant
starts (\fit{TCB1}: 600 iterations, 13.6\,h, still in its first barrier stage) \taskref{261006-diagnosis}. The question
(Luca, 10-06): which recipe holds the physical region exactly, or to a negligible violation, and reaches the same minimum
from any start in predictable time? After the diagnosis it was narrowed: keep trust-krylov, and try a parameter-level or
few-line fix before any reparametrisation. On 10-08 a second question was
added: why does the projected-\ptll\ saturated sub-fit take about 5\,h, a cost that toy-calibrated goodness-of-fit tests
pay once per toy?

\section{Setup and method}

\paragraph{Fits.} All fits are on real data with \alphas\ blinded, in the nominal configuration: card~A (2D
$\ptll\times\yll$, Z$\to\mu\mu$), the SCETlib-AD param model on the $|Y|\le2.5$ subset of the \code{pdf62_y35_260921}
cache, the native lattice CS term, $\lnu4\equiv0$, TMD priors pinned. The wall adds $k\sum_iP(x_i)$ to the NLL, with
$x_i$ the violation of physical-damping condition $i$ in that condition's own units (e.g.\ $L_2(Y)=\lambda_2+\delta\lambda_2Y^2\ge0$
at $|Y|=0$ and 2.5, $\lnu2\ge0$, $3\lambda_\infty^2\lambda_4+L_2^3\ge0$), $k=e^{2\tau}$, $\tau=8$, margin 0. Fit names:
\fit{NOMSTIFF} is the converged reference walled fit. \fit{CENS03R} is a restart of \fit{CENS03}, stopped mid-crawl
after 260 iterations (4.5\,h) at $+430$\,NLL, on the $L_2(|Y|{=}2.5)$ face, $-0.175\,\sigma$ in \alphas\ from
\fit{NOMSTIFF} \taskref{261008-c2-wall-test}; \fit{C2A} (\Ctwo) and \fit{R2A} (\relu) restart from it at $\tau=8$. \fit{LATB8} is a walled lattice-term fit; \fit{SATB8}
(\relu), \fit{SATC2} (\Ctwo), \fit{SATB8SA} (\relu, seeded with a previous sub-fit via \code{--saturatedSeed}~\code{all},
which toys cannot do) and \fit{SATP} (\relu, preconditioned) are its projected-\ptll\ saturated sub-fits (39 free bin
scales, \code{-m}~\code{Project}~\code{ch0}~\code{ptll}), started at \fit{LATB8}'s minimum.

\paragraph{Diagnosis.} The crawl was studied from the fit logs and a quadratic surrogate (the real data-only Hessian,
$3719^2$, the five exact wall conditions, and an instrumented copy of scipy's trust-region loop), which reproduces two
census fits' iteration counts and \fit{CENS03}'s non-convergence \taskref{261006-diagnosis}. The sub-fit was studied
from its logs and one diagnostic job computing dense Hessians at its start ($H_s$), its end ($H_e$) and near the end,
with plain CG on those matrices standing in for trlib's Krylov solve \taskref{261008-saturated-subfit-diagnosis}.

\paragraph{The \Ctwo\ ramp} (built by the study, Fig.~\ref{fig:penalty}). With $x$ the violation and $d$ the ramp width,
\begin{equation}
  P(x)=\begin{cases}0 & x\le0\\ x^3/(3d) & 0<x<d\\ x^2-dx+d^2/3 & x\ge d\end{cases}
  \qquad\text{against}\qquad P_{\mathrm{relu}^2}(x)=\max(x,0)^2 .
\end{equation}
$P$, $P'$, $P''$ are continuous; a step crossing the face by $\Delta x\ll d$ pays $k\Delta x^3/(3d)$ instead of
$k\Delta x^2$. The width is set per condition, $d=\tilde\delta/s$, with $\tilde\delta=10^{-3}$ in the NP exponent at
$b_\mathrm{max}=12.6\,\mathrm{GeV}^{-1}$ (the largest $b_T$ the cache reaches) and $s$ the exponent per unit of the
condition there: $d=3.15\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{GeV}^2$ on $L_2$, $6.3\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{GeV}^2$ on \lnu2
\taskref{261008-c2-wall-test}. Being softer than \relu, the ramp moves an active face's equilibrium outward, predicted
$v=\sqrt{gd/k}=2.40\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{GeV}^2$ at \fit{NOMSTIFF}'s face (\relu: $9.2\times10^{-7}$). Code: the
\code{smooth=} option of \code{wremnants/postprocessing/scetlib_ad/np_damping_wall.py} (each \code{Condition} carries
its scale $s$), tested by \code{scripts/tests/test_np_damping_wall_c2.py} (78 checks, gradient and HVP against finite
differences, \code{smooth=relu2} bitwise equal to the old wall).

\paragraph{The spectral preconditioner} (rabbit's existing option, unmodified; adopted, not built, by the study).
\emph{Once per fit}, at the minimiser's start point, rabbit computes the dense Hessian $H_s$ (306\,s for the 3759-parameter
sub-fit), diagonalises it, replaces the eigenvalues by their absolute values (floored at machine precision times the
largest), and Cholesky-factorises $|H_s|$ into the linear map $T$ with $T^\top|H_s|T=I$. \emph{At every step}
the minimiser works in $y$, with $x=x_0+Ty$: it sees the gradient $T^\top g$ and Hessian-vector products $T^\top HTv$,
at the cost of two dense matrix-vector products, so per-call costs barely change while the Krylov solves see a
whitened Hessian. The flags used,
\begin{center}\small\verb|--precondition --preconditionParams '.*' --preconditionBlocks none --preconditionTransform spectral|\end{center}
whiten all parameters as one block; rabbit's default scope covers only the unconstrained ones. In a saturated test the deep-copied
sub-fitter builds its own $T$ at the sub-fit's start. Code: \code{rabbit/fitter.py} (\code{_build_preconditioner}) and
\code{rabbit/preconditioner.py}, rabbit 2a59246 \taskref{261008-saturated-subfit-diagnosis}.

\section{Results}

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{The \Ctwo\ wall removes the crawl on the real fit and does not move the minimum}{The C2 wall removes the crawl on the real fit and does not move the minimum}}

\caveat{one real start, a snapshot of a fit stuck mid-crawl; \fit{C2A} and \fit{R2A} share node load, cache and
settings, and differ only in the wall. \fit{CENS03R} ran three days earlier on the full cache with different stall flags.}

From the \fit{CENS03R} snapshot, \fit{C2A} reaches the \fit{NOMSTIFF} loss to $10^{-6}$ in 46 iterations and 1.11\,h of
minimiser time. \fit{R2A}, the same command with \relu, was still at $+430$\,NLL after 219 iterations and 1.69\,h and was
stopped by hand; \fit{CENS03R} itself had spent 260 iterations and 4.5\,h there (Fig.~\ref{fig:crawl})
\taskref{261008-c2-wall-test}. The log signature agrees: 78\,\% of \fit{R2A}'s consecutive accepted steps have a gain
ratio near 1, the period-2 lock of Sec.~\ref{sec:mech}, against 7.5\,\% for \fit{C2A}, whose steps double instead as the
radius grows. Every check lands on its prediction (Table~\ref{tab:c2a}): the ramp holds the active face
$1.5\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{GeV}^2$ further out than \relu, and the active-face slope maps that onto
$4\times10^{-6}\,\sigma$ in \alphas, which is what is seen. \textbf{Physics read:} \Ctwo\ changes the minimiser's path,
not the measurement. The face is held with no margin, at $7.6\times10^{-4}$ of the NP exponent at $b_\mathrm{max}$, inside
the $10^{-3}$ tolerance; a \Ctwo--\relu\ loss offset of $\sim10^{-5}$ is irrelevant for any test statistic.

\subsection{Full-scope spectral preconditioning makes the saturated sub-fit about three times faster}
\label{sec:satp}

\caveat{one configuration (\fit{LATB8}), one start (the data); toys have a shorter valley ($q\sim39$ each), so the gain
there is not measured. Node load differed between runs (cost per HVP 6.1--9.9\,s): compare the counts, and the
per-iteration panel of Fig.~\ref{fig:satp}. \fit{SATP} is \fit{SATB8}'s command with only the preconditioner flags added
(and \code{smooth=relu2} written out, since \Ctwo\ had become the default).}

\fit{SATP} reaches \fit{SATB8}'s minimum with 614 Hessian-vector products (HVPs) instead of 2150, 71 loss+gradient
evaluations instead of 156, and 69 iterations instead of 158 (Table~\ref{tab:satp}, Fig.~\ref{fig:satp}). Its minimiser
ran 1.32\,h plus the 306\,s preconditioner Hessian, against 5.82\,h; converted to \fit{SATB8}'s per-call costs that is
1.8\,h against 5.82\,h, about $3\times$ at equal load, and against \fit{SATC2} at similar load 1.40\,h against 4.08\,h. The
answer is unchanged: final loss $1.7\times10^{-10}$ below \fit{SATB8}'s, $q=78.40/39$ in both, converged vectors equal to
$4\times10^{-8}$ in the internal coordinates \taskref{261008-saturated-subfit-diagnosis}. The \Ctwo\ wall, by contrast,
leaves the sub-fit's work unchanged (2141 against 2150 HVPs, the same path iteration for iteration)
\taskref{261008-c2-wall-test}: the cost is not the wall.

\paragraph{Why it works.} Freeing the \ptll\ shape frees the parameters it constrained: \alphas\ is 97.9\,\% degenerate
with the 39 bin scales ($\rho^2=0.979$), and the sub-fit minimum lies $9.6\,\sigma$ (of the main fit) away in \alphas,
with \lnu2, TNPs and luminosity moving by 1.6--3.5$\,\sigma$ while the scales absorb a smooth tilt from 0.908 to 1.106.
The Hessian has $\kappa=1.3\times10^{10}$ at the start and $2.0\times10^9$ at the end, and in raw coordinates the late
Krylov solves need up to $\sim$270 HVPs, each gaining only 35--40\,\% of what is left \taskref{261008-saturated-subfit-diagnosis}.
The obstacle is the \emph{correlation} between \alphas\ and the scales, not their normalisation. The scales' own block is
well conditioned ($\kappa=2.2$); rescaling each parameter (Jacobi) lowers $\kappa$ to $5.5\times10^7$ but leaves the
correlated soft direction and makes CG \emph{slower} (682 HVPs to $10^{-6}$); whitening only the scale block, or rabbit's
default scope, rotates part of the problem and gives $\kappa\sim10^{12}$. Only whitening all parameters together removes
the coupling: $\kappa=1.2\times10^7$ and 77 HVPs, against 290 unpreconditioned (Fig.~\ref{fig:scope}). What remains is
the drift of the soft \alphas\ curvature along the curved valley away from the start-built $T$: in \fit{SATP} the steps are
large until $q_\mathrm{left}\sim4$ (11 iterations, 4\,min), then the tail converges linearly again, but with solves of at
most 93 HVPs instead of 267; 40\,\% of its time is in four solves below $q_\mathrm{left}=0.01$
\taskref{261008-saturated-subfit-diagnosis}.

\paragraph{A looser stop is not safe.} The previous version of this note proposed \code{--minimizerGtol}~0.03 under the
preconditioner to cut the tail. It is dropped: rabbit does not log $\|g_y\|$, and in the start-built frame the soft
direction's curvature drifts so far that a model bound maps $\|g_y\|=0.03$ onto a stop error anywhere between
$\Delta q\sim10^{-7}$ and 0.86; a guaranteed $\Delta q\le0.01$ would need $\lesssim3\times10^{-3}$. Stopping at $q_\mathrm{left}=0.01$ would have saved 37\,\% of \fit{SATP}'s time, but safely only with an EDM-type
stop or a mid-fit rebuild of $T$; a stall-window stop (\code{--earlyStopping} \code{--stallRelTol}), replayed on the logs,
fires on false plateaus with $q$ errors of 0.36--20 \taskref{261008-saturated-subfit-diagnosis}.
\textbf{Physics read:} the preconditioner is a pure reparametrisation; the saturated $q$ and its $p$-value are unchanged,
and a toy calibration of the projected test becomes about three times cheaper per toy, at one extra Hessian
(300--900\,s) per fit.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{The crawl is a limit cycle of scipy's trust-radius rule at a C$^1$ face}{The crawl is a limit cycle of scipy's trust-radius rule at a C1 face}}
\label{sec:mech}

\caveat{the radius-level mechanism comes from the surrogate (nominal configuration only); the real logs record only the
loss, and show the same signature.}

scipy grows the trust radius only if the gain ratio $\rho>0.75$ \emph{and} the step reaches the trust boundary; it shrinks
it if $\rho<0.25$. \relu\ is only C$^1$, so a quadratic model built on the physical side of a face has no wall curvature:
its step crosses the face and the penalty eats about 30\,\% of the predicted gain ($\rho\approx0.71$), no growth. The model
built on the violating side contains the wall; its step lands just back inside, interior, with $\rho\approx1$: again no
growth. The radius freezes (scaling as $1/k$) and the loss falls by a constant amount per iteration
(Fig.~\ref{fig:zigzag}). Gain ratios near 1 make up 40--44\,\% of the step pairs in \fit{CENS03} and \fit{CMR1A}, 1--9\,\%
in normally converged census fits. The lock-in worsens with stiffness: on the surrogate 1 of 5 starts locks in at
$\tau=8$, 2 at $\tau=9$, and 3 do not converge in 20\,000 iterations at $\tau=10$. With the active face eliminated
exactly the same Hessian converges from every start (9--45 iterations), and the \Ctwo\ ramp removes the lock-in from all
six surrogate starts \taskref{261006-diagnosis}.

\section{Robustness, systematics and caveats}

The real-fit evidence for \Ctwo\ is one start; cold and perturbed starts were covered only on the quadratic, nominal-only
surrogate, and the study's benchmark of six real starts was never run. The \Ctwo\ ramp needs a constant scale per
condition, so the interior discriminants of the \code{tanh_6} CS form keep \relu\ by default and can still crawl.

The preconditioning now applies to \emph{every} fit through \code{fitterAD.sh}, the main fit included, where it has not
been measured. On the \relu\ surrogate, full data-Hessian whitening built at a feasible start helped 2 of 5 starts and
hurt 3 (e.g.\ \fit{cold\_000} 95$\to$1181 iterations), because the start Hessian has no wall curvature
\taskref{261006-diagnosis}; with the \Ctwo\ wall the combination is untested. Being a reparametrisation it cannot move a
converged minimum, only the cost.

Two problems are untouched. In the no-lattice configuration with \lnu4\ free, \fit{CMR1B} leaks through the $\lnu4\ge0$
wall to $\gamma_\nu(b_\mathrm{max})=+1.94$ (anti-damped): the data curvature along \lnu4\ there ($7.6\times10^8$) is 40
times the wall's $2k$, so no practical stiffness is exact \taskref{261006-diagnosis}; on the ordinary faces the $\tau=8$
wall leaks only $10^{-4}$--$3\times10^{-4}$ in the exponent at $b_\mathrm{max}$. In the saturated sub-fit \lnu4\ also runs
onto its face, with a leak of $-1.09\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{GeV}^4$ that was not chased \taskref{261008-saturated-subfit-diagnosis}.
On the surrogate, restart-on-stall did not cure the crawl (it never fires, or falls back into the cycle);
$\tau$-continuation ($\tau=5$, then 8 warm) did, but costs a second stage, was never run on a real fit, and was superseded
by \Ctwo\ \taskref{261006-diagnosis}.

\section{Conclusions and open items}

The study is closed (Luca, 2026-10-09) with its goal met. For the \alphas\ measurement nothing moves: \Ctwo\ and \relu\
walls give the same minimum to $4\times10^{-6}\,\sigma$ and the same saturated tests, and the preconditioner changes no
minimum. What changes is the cost: walled fits should finish in predictable time from one process and one cache load, and
a projected saturated sub-fit, the per-toy unit of a toy-calibrated GoF test, costs about a third of what it did. The
following are \textbf{open but parked}; nobody is on them:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Variable projection} of the 39 saturated scales ($s_j=N_j/\nu_j$ inside the loss, which straightens the
    valley; a rabbit change of $\sim$1 day, estimated 3--10$\times$ with the preconditioner, the least certain estimate).
  \item \textbf{A mid-fit preconditioner rebuild} (against the drifting \alphas\ curvature behind \fit{SATP}'s tail), and
    \textbf{an EDM-style stop} (rabbit would need to log $\|g_y\|$ or the EDM), to take the 37\,\% tail safely.
  \item \textbf{\Ctwo\ from a fully cold start} on a real fit; \textbf{preconditioning on toys}, to be measured in the toy
    campaign, and on the main fit (Sec.~4).
  \item The \lnu4\ notch: exact fixes (square-root bound basis, 1--2\,d; active set, 3--5\,d) remain parked.
\end{itemize}
